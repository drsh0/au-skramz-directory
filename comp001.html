au.skramz.directory comp001

The emo/diy scene in so called Australia has grown too much to be ignored, and as such it has been more than enough time for the aus skramz directory to make a sampler CD.

As the scene here is both diverse sound and location wise, it would be impossible to create a collection of songs that could both properly represent the scene & fit onto a CD. Nonetheless we have curated a 12-artist strong playlist. The bands included in this compilation have either been friends to us, creating interesting sounds, performing energetic and unforgettable live shows, strengthening their local scenes, or perhaps doing a combination of the above. To find out which is which, you're just going to have to listen & check out these bands, and then make up your own mind.

This compilation was made for (and first for sale at) the Aus Skramz Directory Stall @ Parramatta Lanes, which ran on the 22nd of October 2026. The songs on this compilation include demos & already released songs. All rights to the songs are retained with the original holders and not the Aus Skramz Directory, Some of these demos may be released later down the line and have changes to the versions included here, some may never see the light of day afterwards if the artists choose so. This compilation will only be released physically, and once the one run is sold out, there will not be future plans to release more. All sales from Parramatta Lanes will be directed towards FBI Radio (who's program 704w High Street has generously invited us as a stall) & sydneymusicdotnet (who without them, there may not be an aus skramz directory)
