<p>Corrigan isn't a band with much history to talk about, but there's enough connections and documentation there that I think it's worth doing a little write-up. Formed in 2025 
from regulars in the season three scene (their <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DMSnRI5TNg5">announcement pic</a> was in front of season three), the band featured Ollie, Ekavir, 
Darren (Who just so happens to be a part of the skramz directory team) & Chloe. The band would play literally two shows, one at <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Ppr6nrpQTU">Ithaca Hall</a>, and a show in a kitchen. After this the band 
would unfortunately break up.
<br>
<p>You might be thinking, that doesn't sound like much to write-up about, and you'd be right, but there are some cool connections which are worth more than the little editor's note at the bottom of the artist card. 
So here's what the members have been up to since:
<ul>
  <li>Chloe continues to play in <a href="https://www.instagram.com/lavenderthreat/">Lavender Threat</a></li>
  <li>Darren would temporarily join Ollie & Ekavir in <a href="https://www.instagram.com/tallworkclub/">Tall Work</a>, as well as working on <a href="https://www.instagram.com/ffozine/">ffozine</a></li>
  <li>Ekavir would also create their noise project <a href="https://www.instagram.com/deonoise__/">Deo</a></li>
  <li>Ollie would also go on to play in <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DUh-W3nAHmh/">Kathleen Turner Overdrive</a></li>
</ul>
