<p>Formed in ~2022 whilst in high school, WeWillLookBackAtYouAndLoveYouForever!!!!! (or wwlbayalyf, as I'm going to type for the rest of this) is the project of Jude & Sholto. Despite the 
absolutely batshit insane nature of this band's music, the duo's path in the Australian skramz scene is both prolific and interesting. For those who were not around for the <a href="https://www.instagram.com/deapfthpoprecords/">Deapfth Pop Records</a> Era, 
may this write-up be a glimspe into what you missed.
<br>
<p>The only way to start with wwlbayalyf is <a href="https://wewilllookbackatyouandloveyouforever.bandcamp.com/album/yabby-time">Yabby Time!!!!!</a>, in my opinion still one of the craziest releases 
in the aus skramz folklore. Outside of the cartoon references & internet memes that define that time of shitposting, the album is filled with a harsh staticky instrumentation & screaming that would be a staple
of the "Claustrophobia Pop" genre. (Check out other artists like Salem's Corydalis & Elizabeth Whitington for examples, both major players in Deapfth Pop as well). On a fun note as well, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/CsAbbC-yo5X">Vinyls</a> were 
made for this album. I personally bought one and had to send the money to their mum's paypal, which is still an all time story. Despite releasing this album in June of 2022, wwlbayalyf would not play their first show until over a year later, in which they would open 
for both <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/CsAbbC-yo5X">keratin & freezer</a>. A very much confirmed rumour is that wwlbayalyf ate a whole lemon (skin included) during the set.
